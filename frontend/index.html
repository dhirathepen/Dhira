I'm Mohith, writing under the pen name Dhira. I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning new skills, and taking on challenges — just for fun.

I am firmly committed to the philosophy of being a jack of all trades, master of none — but often better than a master of one.

My curiosity often leads me to different fields and interests, giving me the opportunity to learn from a wide range of experiences. And writing is always one of them.
